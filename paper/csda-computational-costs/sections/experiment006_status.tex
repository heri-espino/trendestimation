\section{Evidence status and pre-registered comparisons}\label{sec:status}
This section fixes the separation between historical measurements and the prospective experiment. The archived Windows timing corpus in Section~\ref{sec:results} covers $n=24,48$ and $d\leq5$, whereas Experiment006 expands to seven series lengths and all six positive difference orders. The two data sources must not be pooled into one purported factorial cell or used to construct an unmeasured empirical complexity exponent.

Our first hypothesis is that a banded Cholesky plus selected-inverse pipeline will avoid the quadratic memory and cubic setup costs of full dense spectral or inverse-based approaches when $d$ is fixed. The second is that full SVD will become competitive in configurations with enough repeated candidate scores to amortize its initial decomposition. The third is that one-time translation of a fixed $S$ grid will reduce mapping overhead across independently observed series, with identical selected $S$ values for complete, valid matched grids. The fourth is that higher orders, strong smoothness and nonzero reference means can change the numerical failure pattern enough that a raw elapsed-time ranking becomes misleading without an accuracy gate. These are \emph{testable research hypotheses}, not conclusions extrapolated to unmeasured factor levels.

For the full factorial, the primary observed response is end-to-end time in seconds, conditional on numerical correctness. Secondary responses are setup time, one-time mapping time, score time, peak memory, number of root solves, number of PCG iterations and the fraction of ineligible or failed attempts. When the naive dense baseline cannot run, a speedup against that baseline is \emph{undefined}; it must never be replaced with a synthetic time predicted from $O(n^3)$. Mean and confidence intervals are reported across independent paired blocks rather than treating tightly correlated inner-loop stopwatch samples as independent observations.


\subsection{Independent high-order numerical audit (completed pilot)}
A distinct, immutable Experiment006 audit on the Windows self-hosted
\texttt{PCAG103A-08} runner compared fixed-$\mu$ PLS fits with an
independent 85-decimal-digit dual banded-Cholesky reference, including
a 55-versus-85-digit precision-convergence check. The source and
all 112 backend-level observations are preserved in GitHub branch
\texttt{results/experiment006-highd-41439239921}, Actions run
\texttt{41439239921}. Its factor levels were $n=128$,
$d\in\{5,6\}$, $\mu=0.15$, two independent frozen seeds, and
$s\in\{0.75,0.85,0.90,0.91,0.95,0.99,1\}$.
The numerical validity threshold here is relative \emph{forward error}
at most $10^{-6}$ against the independent high-precision fit,
not an arbitrary double-precision normal-equation residual.

The audit demonstrates why a small double-precision KKT residual does
\emph{not} establish a small forward error at high difference order and
high smoothness. Consequently, Experiment006 now exposes an explicitly
named \texttt{guarded\_svd\_cholesky} benchmark policy: shift-free full
SVD for $d\geq5$, and banded Cholesky for $d\leq4$.
Its SVD setup and memory costs are fully included; dense resource
guards make it ineligible at very large $n$. This policy is empirically
supported in the present restricted $n=128$ audit, not a universal
large-series stable solution.

The subsequent guarded Windows pilot, Actions run
\texttt{41439589986}, preserved 80/80 valid observations in the
$d=2$, $\mu=0$ GCV slice, and 56/80 valid observations in the
$d=6$, $\mu=0.15$ CV slice. The 16 newly added guarded observations
were valid, while the prior 24 failures from unguarded methods were
retained. Neither this pilot nor the independent accuracy audit
supplies the requested seven-length complete factorial or
publication-grade speedup confidence intervals.

This distinction also guides manuscript revision. Future Experiment006 results will be inserted only after their raw timing CSV, environment metadata, numerical validity summary, and source revision have been committed to the repository. Until then, the present document is a mathematically developed \emph{working draft} with a completed historical benchmark, not a completed seven-length computational study.
