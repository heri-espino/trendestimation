\section{Completed multi-backend experiments}\label{sec:experiment010}
This section reports only the experiments completed and archived by 3 November
2026. The planned larger factorial is not presented as measured, and the
historical small-series results in Sections~\ref{sec:design}--\ref{sec:results}
are not pooled with the newer Windows timing observations.

\subsection{Full-criterion workloads}
The experiment varies series length $n$, finite-difference order $d$,
candidate smoothness count $M$, number of independent series $R$,
criterion $C$ (CV, GCV, BIC, AICc or Gaussian marginal likelihood),
reference mean $\mu$, observation amplitude, native-$S$ versus
pretranslated-$\lambda$ evaluation, and the complete numerical backend.
Three mean policies are compared:
\[
\mu=0,\qquad
\widehat\mu_{\mathrm{data}}=\frac{\mathbf1^\top D_dy}{n-d},\qquad
\mu=c=0.15.
\]
The sample-derived mean is computed separately for each observed series.
It produces conditional plug-in scores, not exact leave-one-out
re-estimation of the mean or joint marginal maximum likelihood.
For the same $(n,d)$, all three policies share the same exact map
$\Lambda_{n,d}(S)$, independent of the observations and mean policy.

All complete-criterion pipelines charge their setup, smoothness mapping,
fit, effective degrees of freedom, leverage and marginal-likelihood
diagnostics as applicable. The guarded SVD/Cholesky policy is composite;
sparse and PCG hybrid pipelines include their extra banded diagnostics.
An incomplete, unavailable or numerically invalid method is never
assigned a fictitious valid execution time.

\subsection{Completed attempts and archival accounting}
All new measurements used the existing non-administrator
\texttt{trendestimation} Conda environment on university Windows 11
runner \texttt{PCAG103A-08}. The committed source SHA, original
\texttt{raw\_times.csv}, per-method criterion scores and failure
statuses, seed, environment and batch summary are preserved in
immutable GitHub result branches. A status-\texttt{ok} timing row
is a method--workload--coordinate--repetition observation,
\emph{not} an independent experimental problem.

\subsection{All ten pipelines under a matched smoothness grid}
Table~\ref{tab:experiment010_methods} compares the corrected
$n=256,d=1$ GCV experiment: $\mu=0$, high input amplitude,
one observed series, and pretranslated $\lambda$ corresponding to
\emph{identical} $S$ candidates for every numerical backend.
The reported number is the median \emph{total cold} time in
milliseconds over two repetitions, including grid mapping,
factorization and the extra criterion diagnostics where required.

Naive dense inversion completed the single-$S$ evaluation but not
the complete 100-point workload within the resource gate.
Optional sparse CHOLMOD was not installed. The guarded policy
uses banded Cholesky at $d=1$ and should be regarded as a composite,
not a separate factorization algorithm. The numerical pipelines
therefore exhibit a \emph{descriptive} reversal: banded Cholesky
led at one $S$, whereas spectral SVD led at 100 candidates.
This is not an established universal computational crossover.

These two-repetition measurements describe one implementation
and host and cannot support a population-level confidence interval.
At $n=256,d=5$, some structured 100-candidate MLE pipelines
did not finish with completely acceptable criterion records,
whereas SVD and the guarded policy did. At $n=512$, 72 timing
records lacked a valid matched spectral reference.
They must remain unverified rather than declared correct.
