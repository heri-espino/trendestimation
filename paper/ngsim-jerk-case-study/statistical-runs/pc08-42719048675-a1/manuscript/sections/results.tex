\subsection{Three-point $d=3$ continuation versus smoothing splines}
A focused, matched follow-up compares the third-difference recurrence in Eq.~\eqref{eq:d3-recurrence} with the original 25-point continuation using exactly the same archived $d=3$ trend fits and in-sample GCV selections. Conventional natural cubic smoothing splines independently select their penalty by GCV on the 80 training positions. The new numerical results were computed on the named self-hosted Windows runner; original experimental records were left intact.

Smaller values are preferable.}

The three-point recurrence performs best at the 0.5-s horizon and nearly matches the GCV spline's quadratic continuation at 1\,s (0.2958 versus 0.2951\,m). At 2\,s the latter has lower observed-future RMSE (0.8085 versus 1.2998\,m). The relative merit of a nonzero third-difference reference depends on the forecast rule: it improves the local 25-point continuation on these observations but does not improve the three-point recurrence. These comparisons are historical, exploratory results for only two vehicle identifiers, not independent validation of a population-level advantage or of the accuracy of estimated physical jerk. Raw outcome rows, environment manifests and replication scripts are archived under \texttt{experiments/experiment001}, \texttt{experiment002}, and \texttt{experiment003}.
