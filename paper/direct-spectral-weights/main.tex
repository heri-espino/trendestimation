\title[mode=title]{Direct Smoothness-Constrained Spectral Reconstruction for Penalized Least-Squares Trends: Exact Equivalence, Endpoint Geometry, and Nonzero Difference Means}

\begin{abstract}
Finite-difference penalized least-squares (PLS) trends may be described by the penalty $\lambda\in[0,\infty]$ or by a normalized trace smoothness $s\in[0,1]$. We develop and audit a direct spectral-weight representation in which the numerical reconstruction takes $s$ as its input and does not explicitly form $\lambda$. A single strictly monotone scalar equation determines the coupled shrinkage weights. We prove rank and nullspace properties of the difference operator, an exact characterization of the PLS spectral-weight family, existence and uniqueness of the constrained weight solution, equivalence with the conventional penalty-based estimator, endpoint limits, sensitivity bounds, and formulas for a fixed nonzero mean of differences. For the observed-difference plug-in mean, we derive the full data-dependent smoother matrix and its effective-degrees-of-freedom correction, which differs from the customary conditional trace. We give a bracketed log-odds numerical algorithm, complexity accounting and falsifiable benchmark protocols. The scalar root is algebraically equivalent to inversion in log-penalty coordinates: this is neither a new regularization family nor an inversion-free algorithm. No numerical superiority is claimed without a matched accuracy--time study.
\end{abstract}
