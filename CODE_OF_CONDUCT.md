# Code of Conduct

This repository is a scientific software collaboration. Contributors must
interact respectfully and professionally in issues, PRs, reviews and related
project spaces.

Harassment, threats, abuse, discriminatory conduct, disclosure of others'
private information, and deliberately disruptive activity are unacceptable.
Technical disagreement and rigorous criticism are welcome when focused on
the evidence and work.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive contributions, and restrict
participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to a
repository maintainer rather than including personal details in public
issues. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with reasonable confidentiality.
